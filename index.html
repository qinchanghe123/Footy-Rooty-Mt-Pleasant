FAQs

Before your visit.

A few simple answers to help you feel prepared, comfortable, and confident before arriving.

Please arrive about 5–10 minutes early so you have time to check in, settle in, and choose your preferred service without feeling rushed.

If you have an injury, recent surgery, or a medical condition, please consult your healthcare provider first and let us know before your session begins.

Please call as soon as possible if you need to cancel or reschedule. Advance notice helps us make the appointment time available for another guest.

Parking is typically available near the location. Please allow a few extra minutes during busy times so you can arrive calmly before your appointment.
